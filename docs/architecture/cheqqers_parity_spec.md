## 1. Executive Summary & Architectural Audit

This specification documents the complete reverse-engineering of the official JavaScript tournament engine ([`local-cheqqers-server.js`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/local-cheqqers-server.js) + [`@quantumplayed/quantum-game-engine`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/node_modules/@quantumplayed/quantum-game-engine/dist/EntanglementEngine.js)) for **Quantum Cheqqers (`boardSize = 6` or `8`, `startRows = 2` or `3`, `quantumness = 0..3`, tournament default `boardSize = 6, quantumness = 2`)** and compares it line-by-line against the existing Python stack ([`src/env/quantum_checkers.py`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/quantum_games_rl_v2/src/env/quantum_checkers.py), [`src/env/checkers/game.py`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/quantum_games_rl_v2/src/env/checkers/game.py), [`src/env/checkers/board.py`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/quantum_games_rl_v2/src/env/checkers/board.py), and [`server.py`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/quantum_games_rl_v2/server.py#L375-L614)).

### Summary of Critical Findings
1. **Different Quantum Physics Engine**: The official JS server uses `@quantumplayed/quantum-game-engine` (`QuantumEntity`, `JointQuantumSystem`, `EntanglementEngine`, `Operations`) where each playable dark square is a 2-level qubit (`0 = EMPTY`, `1 = OCCUPIED`) with purely real transition amplitudes (`0.70710678`) at `quantumness = 1, 2` and unitary `conditionalInteract` (`CCX` + `CX` shifts) for captures across arbitrary entangled subsystems. By contrast, [`src/env/checkers/game.py`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/quantum_games_rl_v2/src/env/checkers/game.py#L271-L489) replays move histories through Cirq with complex `1j` matrices and `cirq.S` phase gates, and **forcefully collapses** whenever a quantum piece attacks or attacks an already-entangled piece ([`game.py:190-212`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/quantum_games_rl_v2/src/env/checkers/game.py#L190-L212)).
2. **Different Checkers Ruleset (Flying Queens + Backward Men Captures + Maximum Capture Depth)**: The official JS engine implements international/Italian-hybrid draughts rules:
   - Regular pieces (men) move forward 1 square diagonally (`dy === forwardDirection`), but **capture in all 4 diagonal directions** (both forward and backward, [`local-cheqqers-server.js:291`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/local-cheqqers-server.js#L291)).
   - Crowned pieces (**Flying Queens**) slide any number of empty squares along all 4 diagonals and jump distant enemies, landing on any subsequent square with `EMPTY > 0.001` along the ray ([`local-cheqqers-server.js:297-341`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/local-cheqqers-server.js#L297-L341)).
   - When multiple capture moves exist (`takeMoves.length > 1`), only captures achieving the **maximum capture chain depth** (`calculateMaxCaptureDepth`, [`local-cheqqers-server.js:231-243, 371-402`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/local-cheqqers-server.js#L231-L243)) are legal.
   - By contrast, [`src/env/checkers/board.py:84-130`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/quantum_games_rl_v2/src/env/checkers/board.py#L84-L130) only allows 1-step king moves, forbids backward captures by regular pieces, and omits `calculateMaxCaptureDepth`.
3. **Destructive Tournament State Reconstruction & Coordinate Rotation Bug**: [`server.py:527-545, 570-588`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/quantum_games_rl_v2/server.py#L527-L545) discards `data['quantumStates']` and `sq['probability']`, turning every superposition piece into a 100% classical piece, and applies `rot_idx = num_active - 1 - idx` (which reverses both rows and columns instead of just flipping `visualRow`, mirroring the board left-to-right).

## 3. Exact Mathematical & Logical Specification of `OfficialCheqqersEnv`

### 3.1 Coordinate System & Square Indexing
For board size $S \in \{4, 6, 8, 10\}$ (default $S = 6$, `start_rows` $= (S // 2) - 1 = 2$):
- Total playable squares: $N = \lfloor S^2 / 2 \rfloor$ ($18$ for $6\times 6$, $32$ for $8\times 8$).
- Squares per row: $K = \lfloor S / 2 \rfloor$ ($3$ for $6\times 6$, $4$ for $8\times 8$).
- `get_row_col(index)` (exact match with [`local-cheqqers-server.js:196-205`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/local-cheqqers-server.js#L196-L205)):
  $$\text{visualRow} = \lfloor \text{index} / K \rfloor, \quad \text{row} = (S - 1) - \text{visualRow}, \quad \text{colInRow} = \text{index} \bmod K$$
  $$\text{col} = \begin{cases} 2 \cdot \text{colInRow} & \text{if } \text{row} \bmod 2 == 0 \\ 2 \cdot \text{colInRow} + 1 & \text{if } \text{row} \bmod 2 == 1 \end{cases}$$
- `get_index(col, row)` (exact match with [`local-cheqqers-server.js:191-194`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/local-cheqqers-server.js#L191-L194)):
  $$\text{visualRow} = (S - 1) - \text{row}, \quad \text{index} = \text{visualRow} \cdot K + \lfloor \text{col} / 2 \rfloor$$

### 3.2 Direct Sparse Quantum Subsystem Representation
We implement pure Python/NumPy classes matching `@quantumplayed/quantum-game-engine` bit-for-bit with zero Cirq dependency:
- **`QuantumSquare`** (matches `QuantumEntity`, $d = 2$, states `0 = EMPTY`, `1 = OCCUPIED`):
  - Holds `amplitudes: dict[int, complex]` when unentangled, or points to `entangled_system: Optional[JointSystem]` when entangled.
- **`JointSystem`** (matches `JointQuantumSystem` in [`EntanglementEngine.js:4-404`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/node_modules/@quantumplayed/quantum-game-engine/dist/EntanglementEngine.js#L4-L404)):
  - Holds `entities: list[QuantumSquare]` (length $m$) and sparse `joint_amplitudes: dict[int, complex]` where key $k \in [0, 2^m - 1]$ encodes the $m$-bit basis state $(s_0, s_1, \dots, s_{m-1}) \in \{0, 1\}^m$ via big-endian bit composition:
    $$k = \sum_{j=0}^{m-1} s_j \, 2^{m - 1 - j}$$
  - Bitwise operations replace array allocations in `decomposeState` and `composeState`, making `applyToSubspace`, `conditionalInteract`, `getTargetProbabilities`, `measureTarget`, and `factorOutEntity` $>50\times$ faster than Cirq while preserving $< 10^{-9}$ amplitude discrepancy with `EntanglementEngine.js`!
  - Sparsity cleanup (`clean_amplitudes`): drops entries where $|\text{re}| < 10^{-10}$ and $|\text{im}| < 10^{-10}$ ([`Operations.js:3-13`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/node_modules/@quantumplayed/quantum-game-engine/dist/Operations.js#L3-L13)).

### 3.3 Exact Reconstruction from Tournament `/move` Payload (`from_tournament_json`)
Given the JSON dictionary `data` from `local-cheqqers-server.js` (`board`, `quantumStates`, `legalMoves` / `availableMoves`, `player` / `currentTurn` / `currentPlayer`, `settings`):
1. Extract `board_size` (default `6`) and `quantumness` (`game_type`, default `2`).
2. Reconstruct `piece_properties[i]` for every square $i \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ where `board[i]['piece']` is non-null and `board[i]['probability'] > 0.0001`:
   - `color = WHITE (0)` if `'white'` else `BLACK (1)`.
   - `crowned = bool(board[i].get('crowned') or board[i].get('isQueen'))`.
   - Assign distinct `pieceIds = [next_piece_id]`.
3. Reconstruct quantum state amplitudes directly from `data['quantumStates']` ([`local-cheqqers-server.js:812-840`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/local-cheqqers-server.js#L812-L840)):
   - Each entry in `quantumStates` has:
     - Either the officialCheqqers format: `{"type": "entangled" | "independent", "squares": [i_0, ...], "amplitudes": [{"state": "W⊗E...", "re": ..., "im": ...}]}`
     - Or flat basis format: `[{"state": ..., "real"|"re": ..., "imag"|"im": ..., "probability": ...}]`.
   - For `"independent"` on `squares = [i]`: parse each amplitude token (`"E"` $\to 0$, `"W"`, `"WQ"`, `"B"`, `"BQ"` $\to 1$) and set `squares[i].amplitudes[s] = complex(re, im)`.

### 3.4 Exact Born-Rule Branch Enumeration (`get_move_outcomes`)
When `env.get_move_outcomes(move)` is called (where `move` can be a move dict `{"from_index": ..., "to_index": ...}`, a tournament list `[from, to]` / `[a, b, c]`, or an action index `int`):
1. Identify the ordered list of pre-move measurements from `get_involved_squares(move)` ([`local-cheqqers-server.js:545-561`](file:///home/stefano-troffa/Personal_proj/ETQG_2026/Cheqqers/local-cheqqers-server.js#L545-L561)):
   - Each involved square `(sq_idx, role)` is a measurement candidate if `should_measure = (game_type == GameType.SUPERPOSITION) or (role == 'landing')`.
2. Instead of calling `Math.random()`, recursively branch through the sequence of involved squares:
   - At step `idx` in `involvement`:
     - Query current marginal probability $p_1 = P(\text{sq\_idx} = \text{OCCUPIED})$ and $p_0 = 1 - p_1 = P(\text{sq\_idx} = \text{EMPTY})$ on the current branch state.
     - If `not should_measure` or `not (0.001 < p_1 < 0.999)`: no measurement occurs on `sq_idx`; continue to `idx + 1`.
     - Otherwise, `sq_idx` is measured! Enumerate outcome $r \in \{0, 1\}$ with marginal probability $p_r > 10^{-9}$:
       - Clone the branch environment, execute `measure_square(sq_idx, forced=r)` (which collapses `sq_idx` to $r$, normalizes surviving amplitudes by $1/\sqrt{p_r}$, runs `disentangle_collapsed_entities()` and `cleanup_piece_properties()`), and multiply branch probability by $p_r$.
       - **Crucial `moveFailed` Short-Circuit (`local-cheqqers-server.js:559`)**: If `role == 'landing'` and $r == 1$ (`OCCUPIED`), `move_failed = True`! The loop over remaining `involvement` squares terminates immediately (`break`), the move is NOT applied, `turn` flips to the opponent, `multi_jump_square = None`, `jumped_piece_ids_this_turn = []`, `check_win_states()` runs, and this branch `(branch_prob, failed_env)` is emitted!
3. For every surviving branch where `move_failed == False`:
   - Execute the unitary/classical gate (`apply_take_move`, `apply_split_move`, `apply_merge_move`, or `apply_classical_move`).
   - Note: In `GameType.SUPERPOSITION (1)`, `apply_classical_move` (`L712`) and `apply_take_move` (`L735`) also check if `from_index` is still in superposition (though `from_index` has `role == 'source'` and was already measured in the pre-move loop when `gameType == SUPERPOSITION`).
   - Run `apply_phase_pass()`, `cleanup_piece_properties()`, `disentangle_collapsed_entities()`, update `turn`, `multi_jump_square`, `moves_since_take`, `consecutive_queen_moves`, and `check_win_states()`.
4. Normalize branch probabilities so $\sum_k p_k = 1.0$ and return `list[tuple[float, OfficialCheqqersEnv]]` sorted in descending order of probability $p_k$ (required for Star1 Expecti-AlphaBeta pruning!).

### 3.5 Quantum-Aware Tactical Evaluation (`evaluate_state`)
`evaluate_state(perspective_color: int | str) -> float` computes a fast, rich tactical score in bounded range $[-V_{\max}, +V_{\max}]$ ($V_{\max} = 1000.0$ for terminal win/loss, $0.0$ for draw):
1. **Terminal Value**: $+1000.0$ if `perspective` won, $-1000.0$ if opponent won, $0.0$ if draw.
2. **Expected Material (Weighted by Marginal Born Probabilities $p_i$)**:
   - Uncrowned regular piece: $1.00 \cdot p_i$.
   - Crowned Flying Queen: $2.60 \cdot p_i$ (reflecting long-range diagonal control on $6\times 6$ / $8\times 8$).
3. **King-Row Promotion Proximity & Back-Rank Guard**:
   - Uncrowned piece advancement along forward rows: $+0.35 \cdot p_i \cdot (\text{progress}_i)^2$, accelerating as a piece approaches crowning row.
   - Back-rank defense bonus: $+0.18 \cdot p_i$ for uncrowned pieces guarding the home promotion rank (`row == 0` for White, `row == size - 1` for Black) while opponent still has uncrowned pieces.
4. **Central Board Control & Flying Queen Diagonal Dominance**:
   - Centrality bonus $+0.15 \cdot p_i \cdot (1 - \text{dist\_to\_center} / S)$.
   - Queen central-diagonal bonus $+0.20 \cdot p_i$ when a Flying Queen occupies the main diagonal or center squares.
5. **Quantum Superposition & Split Advantage**:
   - Coherent split bonus $+0.08 \cdot p_i (1 - p_i)$ for pieces in superposition ($0.05 < p_i < 0.95$) that threaten multiple diagonals simultaneously and force opponent landing-measurement risk (`moveFailed` trap!).
6. **Tactical Mobility & Capture-Threat Safety**:
   - Active turn / multi-jump tempo bonus: if `multi_jump_square is not None`, $+0.45$ for the jumping player.
   - Immediate capture threat bonus/penalty: checks available capture moves for both sides (using fast `find_moves(is_take=True)` without recursive depth search) and rewards having capture threats ($+0.30$ weighted by attacker/victim coexistence probability) while penalizing hanging pieces exposed to opponent jumps ($-0.35$).
